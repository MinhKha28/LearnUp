# Learning Service (`services/learning-service`)

## 1. Overview
* **Service Name**: `learning-service`
* **Service Classification**: Business Service (3 of 5)
* **Intended Technology**: NestJS (TypeScript)
* **Dedicated Database**: PostgreSQL (`learning_db`)
* **Eureka Service Identifier**: `LEARNING-SERVICE`
* **Implementation Status**: **Skeleton Only** (Implementation intentionally deferred to the NestJS team)

---

## 2. High-Level Domain Ownership (Source of Truth)
The Learning Service is the authority for learner progression and enrollment lifecycles:
* **Enrollments**: Voluntary self-enrollments (`OPTIONAL`) and mandatory assignments (`REQUIRED`).
* **Enrollment State Machine**: Full 7-state lifecycle (`NotStarted`, `InProgress`, `Completed`, `Overdue`, `Exempted`, `Withdrawn`, `Halted`).
* **Progress Tracking**: Lesson progress tracking, material access checks, and course completion percentage calculation.
* **Course Disabled Reaction**: Consumes `CourseDisabled` events to halt active enrollments.
* **Completion Broadcasting**: Publishes `CourseCompleted` event via Transactional Outbox upon reaching 100% completion.

---

## 3. Strict Boundary Rules
* **No Direct Cross-Database Access**: Must never connect directly to other services' databases.
* **Curriculum Structure**: Syllabus structure and lesson contents are owned by **Course Service** (`course_db`).
* **Quiz Grading**: Scoring and quiz attempt evaluation are owned by **Assessment Service** (`assessment_db`).
* **Certificate Issuance**: Certificates are generated by **Achievement & Reporting Service** (`achievement_reporting_db`).
* **Deferred Implementation**: Contains no Java/Spring Boot code. Will be implemented independently by the NestJS team.
